# Limit 5 per item

A shopper can buy at most 5 of any one item.

- In the cart, an item's "+" button is disabled once its quantity is 5.
- At 5, the item's line shows the note "Limit 5 per item".
- On the Products page, "Add to cart" for an item that's already at 5 in the cart shows the message "You can buy at most 5 of this item", and the cart stays as it was.
