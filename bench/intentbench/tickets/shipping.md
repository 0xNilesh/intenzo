# Free shipping progress

Orders of $75.00 or more ship free; below that, shipping is $5.00.

- The order summary has a "Shipping" line: $5.00 below $75.00, and "Free" at $75.00 or more.
- Below $75.00, a note under the summary says how much more is needed: "Add $25.00 more for free shipping" for the $50.00 cart.
- At $75.00 or more, the note says "You've got free shipping!".
- The total includes shipping: $55.00 for the $50.00 cart, and just the subtotal when shipping is free.
